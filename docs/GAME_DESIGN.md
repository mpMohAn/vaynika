# Game Design — v0.1

## Vision
A readable, responsive 2D cinematic action-platformer inspired by the *feel* of classic DOS-era movement adventures, without copying their copyrighted art or levels. Set in fictional Suvarnagiri, drawing respectfully on historical Deccan visual motifs.

## Protagonist
Queen **Vaynika**: 27, human, slender and athletic, long black hair, warm Indian complexion, expressive brown eyes. An intelligent ruler and skilled but mortal fighter. Royal costume: crimson and ivory. Warrior costume: practical black and bronze armor, minimal gold.

## Core loop
Explore a palace room → navigate architecture → duel a guard → reach an exit → progress the story.

## First milestone
One playable room: move, jump, block, sword attack, enemy health, player health, restart, exit trigger, and touch controls. The prototype uses placeholder art until the character master is approved.

## Gameplay principles
- Responsive controls and readable attack tells
- Gravity, grounded movement and collision
- No magical powers; momentum and skill matter
- Fair combat windows, brief invulnerability after taking damage
- Keyboard first; mobile touch support for early testing

## Future
Climbing, ledge grabs, traps, checkpoints, multi-room palace escape, cinematic transitions, music, accessibility settings, controller support, localization, and enemy variety.

## Scope guard
Do not add a full campaign, 3D engine, multiplayer or asset-heavy cinematic cutscenes before the movement and combat feel right.
